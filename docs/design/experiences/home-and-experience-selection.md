# Home and experience selection

**Status:** Current  
**Last updated:** 30 September 2026

## Diksoochi landing

After a profile is active, Diksoochi is the application landing experience. A continuously but gently animated Krishna flute replaces the visible page title while a visually hidden heading preserves screen-reader and navigation semantics. Reduced-motion preferences stop the animation. The profile pill stays visible at the upper right.

The page prioritizes action: **Continue your journey** appears first, followed by **Choose an experience**. A **Your journey** section at the bottom contains Know, Able and Use. About Gitaverse and installation remain available below it.

Know counts distinct chapters and shlokas only after at least ten seconds of meaningful visible engagement. Brief navigation does not count. Able and Use display honest unfolding states until explicit self-assessment and private application experiences exist; they are never inferred from time or playback.

## Choices

The **Choose an experience** section is part of Diksoochi. Gita 700 is selectable and opens the shared player with its renderer. Gita Yoga and Gita Sara are visible as coming-later choices so the intended experience family is understandable without implying they are implemented.

Choosing an experience preserves the profile identifier and preferred content language in application navigation. The profile’s app language governs this interface; `lang` links affect content only. The Home command (`A`) returns from the player to Diksoochi and records it as the resumable location. Direct experience URLs still open the requested experience.

## About

About Gitaverse is available throughout the path from profile setup to the player. It uses the flute as a Krishna-associated visual symbol, links to Gitajyoti and Light of the Self, and displays the deployed application version.
