# Welcome to Rachana

**Status:** Current  
**Last updated:** 10 October 2026

Rachana is the living home for **Gitaverse design details**. It describes the product as users experience it and connects those experiences to the systems that make them work.

## Read by purpose

- Start with [Purpose and principles](design/product/purpose-and-principles.md) for the intent behind Gitaverse.
- Follow [Profiles and first use](design/experiences/profiles-and-first-use.md), [Home and experience selection](design/experiences/home-and-experience-selection.md), [Shared player](design/experiences/shared-player.md), [Gita Yoga](design/experiences/gita-yoga.md), [Gita 700](design/experiences/gita-700.md), and [Gita Sara](design/experiences/gita-sara.md) for the user journey.
- Open [Architecture overview](design/system/architecture-overview.md) and the remaining System pages to understand implementation and operations.
- Visit [Sampada](design/sampada.md) for approved future possibilities.

## Reading convention

Current pages describe implemented behavior. Partial pages identify a deliberate gap. Future ideas remain in Sampada until they are implemented, verified, and incorporated into the current-design pages.
