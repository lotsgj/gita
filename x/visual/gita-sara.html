कर्मण्येवाधिकारस्ते
मा फलेषु कदाचन ।
मा कर्मफलहेतुर्भूः
मा ते सङ्गोऽस्त्वकर्मणि ॥

You have a right to action alone, never to its fruits. Let not the fruits of action be your motive, nor let your attachment be to inaction.

karmaṇy-evādhikāras te
mā phaleṣu kadācana
mā karma-phala-hetur bhūr
mā te saṅgo ’stv akarmaṇi

karmaṇi—in action; adhikāraḥ—right; phaleṣu—in the fruits; saṅgaḥ—attachment; akarmaṇi—to inaction
